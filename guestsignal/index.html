FAQ

Questions owners usually ask.

Do I have to approve every response?

You can approve each response by email or text. If you prefer, safe non-escalated drafts can follow an agreed fallback after the approval window.

Will the replies sound like AI?

No generic replies. Onboarding creates a voice profile for your business, and every edit you make becomes calibration.

What happens with serious complaints?

Health complaints, accusations, discrimination claims, and viral-risk reviews are escalated for human review and are never treated like routine responses.

Is this only for restaurants?

The launch focus is independent Portland restaurants, bars, and hospitality businesses. The same approach can also fit boutique hotels, salons, wellness studios, and specialty retail.
